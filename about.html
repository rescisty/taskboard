About TaskBoard

Why we built a bulletin board for hired work, and who it's for.

The idea

Most commission platforms are built for one kind of work — usually art. TaskBoard started from a simple question: why should a coder, an editor, or a rigger have to use a different site than an illustrator just to get hired for small jobs? TaskBoard is a single board where any task, in any skill, can be posted and picked up.

Think of it less like a storefront and more like a corkboard — tasks get pinned, creators claim what fits them, and both sides track progress until it's done.

Any skill, one board

Art, code, editing, writing, audio, design — one place to post and one place to browse.

Built for small jobs

Not every task needs a full agency. TaskBoard is for the in-between-sized work.

Clear status, always

Every task has a visible status, so nobody's left guessing where things stand.
