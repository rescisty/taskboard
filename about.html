About TaskBoard

A bulletin board for creative work.

TaskBoard is where people with an idea meet people with the skills to make it real. Post what you need, or open the board and pick up work you would love to do.

If you need something made

  1. Post a task with a budget, or message a creator directly.
  2. Talk through the details in a private chat.
  3. When it's done, rate how it went.
Post a Task

If you make things

  1. Set up a profile with your services and portfolio.
  2. Claim open tasks from the board.
  3. Build your reputation with ratings.

What we care about

Clear, honest work

Private conversations
